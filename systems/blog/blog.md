# Blog & Donations System

This document explains the core progression loop of the game, which involves taking photos, uploading them to a blog, and earning donations.

## 1. Concept

This system forms the primary gameplay loop outside of the shooting galleries. The player acts as a paranormal investigator who documents their findings (the enemies) by taking photos and uploading them to a blog for their online followers. The quality of the photos determines the amount of donations received, which acts as the game's main currency.

## 2. The Gameplay Loop

1.  **Investigate & Photograph**: The player explores the world map, entering battle screens to find and photograph enemies using the **Capture Feature**.
2.  **Camera Capacity**: The player's camera can only hold a limited number of photos. Once the camera is full, the player can no longer take new pictures.
3.  **Return to the Trailer**: The player must return to their home base, the "Trailer," which is a special, non-hostile location on the world map.
4.  **Upload to Blog**: Inside the trailer, the player accesses a special UI to review all the photos they have taken. They can then select their best shots to upload to their blog.
5.  **Earn Donations**: After uploading, the player receives donations from their followers. The amount earned will likely depend on the quality of the photos (e.g., clarity, centering of the enemy, enemy rarity).

## 3. The Trailer (Hub)

The trailer is a safe hub area. From here, the player can:

-   Access the Blog UI to upload photos.
-   Potentially upgrade their equipment using the donations they've earned.
-   Manage their inventory.

## 4. Economy & Progression

-   **Currency**: Donations are the primary currency.
-   **Upgrades**: This currency can be used to drive progression. For example, the player could purchase:
    -   A camera with a larger photo capacity.
    -   Better lenses for clearer pictures (leading to more donations).
    -   Upgrades that help with the **Fear Mechanic** or other battle systems.

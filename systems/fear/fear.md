# Fear Mechanic

This document explains the "Fear Mechanic," a feature that adds a layer of risk to the gameplay.

## 1. Concept

The Fear Mechanic introduces a health system where the player's health diminishes when they are in a state of "fear." This creates a sense of urgency and encourages the player to be precise and quick with their actions.

## 2. Trigger

The state of "fear" is triggered when the player's targeting crosshair is positioned directly over an enemy. As long as the crosshair remains on the target, the player is considered to be in a state of fear.

## 3. Effect

While the Fear Mechanic is active, the player's health bar will slowly and continuously decrease. To stop the health loss, the player must move the crosshair off the enemy.

This mechanic creates a risk/reward scenario: aiming at an enemy is necessary to shoot it, but holding the aim for too long will penalize the player.

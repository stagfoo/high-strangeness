# Game Details
- this game is about taking photos of cyrpidis during a road trip with your mum.
- the game loop will be the user travels of a rest area along an america highway (a level) that area will have a shop to buy item upgrades, an area to explore, a comic to view (story). Inside the area to explore it will be 5-10 screens presented as a dungeon crawler which the user clicks forward, right or left or back, each screen will have a chance to spawn an animal or cryptid.
- the battle screen is dark so it will be hard to see the animal or cryptid so the user controls a flash light and camera to take photos of it
- the user has a sleepyness meter that is a timer, the user can buy engery drinks for the shop to increase there time but it also decreases your fear meter
- the fear meter is similar to a health bar, the more scared you are the more likely you will pass out, the player gets scaried the longer they look at a cryptid and some cryptids are large so hard to stop looking at, or they move or make the fear meeter go down faster
- once the player has taken the photos they will return to the rv and upload them to their blog, where people send donations depending on how good the photo is
- the photo rating system will be how much of the monster's hitbox was inside the photo (it would be good if We can add blur to the photo depending the speed of the player movement but not sure
- the end of game when the road trip ends

## Terms
- a system is a group of game mechanics
- a game mechanic is the logic of a small section of a game 
- an example mechanic would be a "you can take photos"
- an example of a system would be "you can have one of many camera, it takes photos, it has a speed limit of how fast it takes a photo, it has a limit of how many photo you can take, photos can be sold"
